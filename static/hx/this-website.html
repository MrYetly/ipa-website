This Website

I started off making this website at a time in my life when I was drifting anti-AI. I liked the idea of writing it all myself, and so I did, until I had to write CSS for the first time. AI started to look a lot more reasonable then. I have a feeling many things on the productive side of my life are going to go through similar vicissitudes. This website is just one part of my attempt to grapple with them.
