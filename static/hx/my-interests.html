My Interests

I am interested in answering the question: what if AI could let me do more of what I really wanted to do?

The arc of technological change is pushing humanity to care more about which problems it solves because AI is giving us the confidence to assume that we can solve any problem we properly pose. I think this will hold true across any field or context we can think of: I do not think that mathematics will die, I think that the way we solve problems will completely change. I don't think coding will die either, it too will change. The most influential force across all of the changes that occur will be the true, deep, and potentially knowable desires of humanity. It is not obvious to me that any of us know what we really need or want, and that we spend the majority of our lives experimenting through trial and error to see if this is what we need or want today, and theorizing what we will need or want tomorrow. No one is really an expert at this in the sense traditionally held by technical communities: expertise here is not a matter of objective truth, it is a matter of tast, faith, and morality. It is more the area of philosophy and religion. Of marketing, if we're being charitable.

So, what are my interests? What do I want and need? Well, the first priority here is my relationships, but since we are discussing the "productive" side of our lives, I'll skip that for now. That leaves two clear things: I want to become wealthy and to solve meaningful problems, not necessariliy in that order. This, of course, is merely kicking the can down the road. How do I want to become wealty? What problems do I want to solve?

Starting with the second question, I have been attracted to serving small businesses for the past year and a half. I started a business to do so. I did this because I am struck by how most of what makes a place charming is derived from small businesses. The main street of a town is "the place to be" because of its restaurants, cafes, barber shops, salons, private practices, gyms, retail stores, I could go on and on. It also strikes me that there may be an economic opportinity in serving these businesses, simply because much of the tech sector has mostly cared about enterprise-level services and products. Not exclusively, but that is where the money is, after all. It seems to me that it would be incredibly satisfying to help small businesses thrive. I have already attempted to make two products for small businesses, AI agents both, and failed on both accounts. Still, this remains a compelling prospect.

As for getting wealthy, I can't help but feel like the advent of AI will make it easier to make money on the stock market. Not easy, easier. Given my existing expertise in econometrics, I plan on learning quite a bit about stochastic calculus, time series forecasting, and machine learning, so I can more fluently complement any alpha-generating ideas I may have. As for the alpha-generating ideas, well that's the hard part. With the help of AI, though, I am now freed up to try and come up with as many ideas as I can, confident that AI will be able to properly falsify them. I am very creative, so this is a greate opportunity to test myself.
