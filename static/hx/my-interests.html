My Interests

I would never do economics, causal inference, or forecasting for free, but in terms of the productive side of my life, I love these things.

In an alternate universe, I have had a job since graduating with my PhD in economics in 2024. In this job, I have slowly built my skillset to become a valuable contributor to a company and subsequently my interests have dovetailed with its.

In this universe, fortune has not been so ostensibly kind. I have struggled to get a job since losing my first seven months after graduating. The labor market has sent me a clear signal that as I stand, I am not that interesting, valuable, or worthy of investment. Needless to say, I disagree, and my interests have evolved accordingly.

I am broadly interested in being a data scientist and an economist. I have already attempted to start a company due to my inability to find a fit for my skills in the labor market. I currently find myself trying again to navigate that market, but I may try to start a company again if the market doesn't have a place for me to exercise my skills.
