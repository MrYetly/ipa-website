Work

  • Prblm Solver. I founded this company to attempt to build AI agents for small businesses. I created two products. The first was one I created from the harness up, writing a lot of the harness code myself in LangGraph and relying on Claude to build the FastAPI web app around it. It was a provenance researcher agent, meant to help professional collectors, vintage stores, antique stores, etc discover the origins of their items. As it turns out, none of those business entities were interested in the product. The second was a tax expense classification helper meant to help small business owners track and categorize their expenses according to Schedule C. After the failure of teh first agent, I realized I should iterate more quickly. One way to do this was to rely on OpenClaw as the harness and focus on creating (hopefully) valuable pipelines the agent could interact with. I achieved this architecture, but again the demand for the product was lacking.
