Favorite Books

This is not an exhaustive list

  • Dune. My favorite book of all time.
  • The Expanse Series. My favorite book series of all time. The first time I read it, I read the first 8 books in 4 months. I am currently in the middle of rereading it, having just finished book 5 again.
  • Children of Time Series. Author Adrian Tchaikovsky has an incredible ability to imagine philosophically interesting and culturally relevant characters, concepts, and scenarios that make my brain happy. A lot of people might just think its weird, but I love it.
  • Narcissus and Goldmund. This book was a big influence on my choise to not go into academia. Great books have a way of discussing and making you think about important topics in ways few other things can, and this on dive deep into a point of view on what it really means to live a life of the mind versus a life of experience.
