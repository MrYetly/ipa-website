I wrote the HTTP server for this website from scrach in C; I wrote the HTML, the CSS, and the Javascript. None of the content on this page is AI generated either.

What's the point of learning these things in the age of AI? Why should I, an economist, a data scientist, an entrepreneur, "waste my time" with these things when an AI could do it all? Isn't that more productive? In the short term, yes it often is, but anything worth achieving isn't short term.

Currenly, AI has no taste. It cannot tell the difference between good and perfect. This is why AIs are not writing the next greate novel every few seconds. Techinically, this is certainly possible: an output window of 250,000 tokens is equivalent to around 350 to 500 pages. It doesn't happen because AIs have no opinions, they have no life experinece telling them to go in a certain direction. The consequence of this for all work is that an AI is in capable of having any kind of vision for what it does.

AI lets you can burst ahead of the limits of your knowledge. Sometimes this is sufficient for success. Most of the time, you end up standing on tenuous ground. The further ahead you burst, the more restricted your ability to go further, to change direction, or even explain where you are, because all around you are things you don't understand.
