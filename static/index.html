Welcome

It takes more than intelligence to do things. Please come in and get to know me, my understanding, and my taste.

Everything you see here was created by me. Some of the technology used to deliver it to your screen is, too. I wrote the HTTP server and the (admittedly minimal) backend logic for this website from scrach in C; I wrote the HTML, the CSS, and the Javascript. None of the content on this page is AI generated either. Some of my other work showcased here contains AI generated content or code, because those works served different purposes. This website is for you and for me, by myself.

I learned these technologies in the age of AI because it has become clear to me that AI is currently still limited by the understanding and taste of those who use it. AI lets you can burst ahead of the limits of your knowledge. Sometimes this is sufficient for success. Most of the time, you end up standing on tenuous ground. The further ahead you burst, the more restricted your ability to go further, to change direction, or even explain where you are, because all around you are things you don't understand. You can't exercise your taste, your opinion, and so you either retreat to where you can take control again, or you push on, entirely dependent on a process whose most salient truth led you to the confusion you are currently in.
